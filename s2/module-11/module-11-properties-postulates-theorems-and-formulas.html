Properties

1. Slicing Principle: A cross-section of a three-dimensional figure is the intersection of the solid and a plane.

2. Perpendicular Plane Slice: A plane that intersects the base at a 90° angle can produce varying cross-sections depending on the height and shape of the solid.

3. Parallel Plane Slice: A plane parallel to the base produces a cross-section congruent (or similar, in the case of pyramids and cones) to the base.

4. Similarity of Cross-Sections: Slices of cones and pyramids parallel to the base are similar in shape to the base.

5. Congruent Cross-Sections in Prisms and Cylinders: Slices parallel to the bases are congruent to the base.

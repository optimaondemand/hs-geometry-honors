Formulas

1. Distance Formula

\(\sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2+(y_2-y_1)^2}\)

where \((x_1,y_1)\) and \((x_2,y_2)\) are the coordinates of two points

2. Midpoint Formula

If you know the coordinates of two endpoints \((x_1,y_1)\) and \((x_2,y_2)\), use the Midpoint Formula.

\(Midpoint=\Large (\frac{x_1+x_2}{2},\frac{y_1+y_2}{2})\)

3. Endpoint when given the Midpoint and One Endpoint

\(x_2=2M_x-x_1\)

\(y_2=2M_y-y_1\)

when Midpoint = \((M_x,M_y)\) and the Endpoint = \((x_1,y_1)\).

4. Section Formula

When given endpoints \((x_1,y_1)\) and \((x_2,y_2)\) and a ratio \(m:n\), use the Section Formula:

\(\Large (\frac{mx_2+nx_1}{m+n},\frac{my_2+ny_1}{m+n})\)

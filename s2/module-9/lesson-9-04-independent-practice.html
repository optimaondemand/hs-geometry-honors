📝 ASSIGNMENT

Print out the PDF here to complete this assignment or write answers and work on a piece of paper to submit.

The assignment shows 5 triangles, each on its own coordinate plane.

Classify each triangle by its side measures: scalene, isosceles, or equilateral. Then classify each triangle by its angle measures: right, acute, or obtuse.

Justify your choices by showing ALL work to show why. You MUST show the work to justify the side measure classification AND the angle measure classification.

Use the charts on the lesson page to help organize your work!!


Expectations Checklist

  • Correctly classify triangle by side lengths (Scalene, Isosceles, Equilateral).

  • Correctly classify triangle by angle measures (Acute, Right, Obtuse).

  • Show all side length work using the Distance Formula.

  • Use the Slope Formula to check for perpendicular sides (only if needed for right triangles).

  • Apply the Pythagorean Theorem and triangle inequalities to justify angle classification.

  • Organize work clearly with labels and calculations shown.

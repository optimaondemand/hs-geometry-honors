📋 CONSTRUCTING AN INSCRIBED TRIANGLE IN A CIRCLE

Objective: In this assignment, you will use a provided PDF containing a circle and a point on the circle, print it out, and construct an accurate inscribed equilateral triangle in the circle. You will document your construction process by recording a video or taking a picture, which you will then upload to the assignment page.

Materials Needed:

  • Printed PDF with the provided line segment
  • Compass
  • Straightedge (ruler without measurement markings)
  • Pencil
  • Recording device (camera or smartphone)


Steps to Complete the Assignment:

  1. Print the PDF:

    • Download and print this PDF with the given circle and point.

  2. Prepare Your Tools:

    • Gather your compass, straightedge, and pencil.

  3. Using the construction steps in the lesson construct an inscribed triangle in the circle.

  4. Label the Points.

  5. Document the Construction:

    • Record a video or take a clear picture of your construction, making sure to show:
      • The 5 arc marks on the circle.
      • The labeled vertices.
      • The drawn triangle using every other arc mark on the circle.

  6. Upload Your Documentation:

    • Upload the video or image of your completed construction to the assignment page. Ensure the file is clear and legible.


Submission Requirements:

  • Video or Image: Ensure your video or image clearly shows the segments, arc marks, circle, and tangents.
  • File Format: Acceptable formats include MP4, MOV, JPG, and PNG.


Grading Criteria:

  • Accuracy: The constructed tangents are truly tangent and accurate
  • Clarity: The video or image must clearly show all steps and labels.
  • Markings: Segments, arc marks, perpendicular bisector, circle showing tangents..
  • Completion: The submission must include all required elements (ray, arc mark, labeled points).


If you have any questions or need further clarification, please contact your instructor. Happy constructing!

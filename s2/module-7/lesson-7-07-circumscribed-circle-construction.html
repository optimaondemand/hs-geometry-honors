📋 CONSTRUCTING A CIRCUMSCRIBED CIRCLE IN A TRIANGLE

Objective: In this assignment, you will use a provided PDF containing a triangle, print it out, and construct an accurate circumscribed circle around the triangle. You will document your construction process by recording a video or taking a picture, which you will then upload to the assignment page.

Materials Needed:

  • Printed PDF with the provided line segment
  • Compass
  • Straightedge (ruler without measurement markings)
  • Pencil
  • Recording device (camera or smartphone)


Steps to Complete the Assignment:

  1. Print the PDF:

    • Download and print this PDF containing the given triangle.

  2. Prepare Your Tools:

    • Gather your compass, straightedge, and pencil.

  3. Construct the Circumscribed Circle

    • Using the construction steps from the lesson, construct the circumscribed circle around the given triangle by following these steps:
      1. Find and construct the perpendicular bisectors of each side of the triangle.
      2. Identify the point where the perpendicular bisectors intersect—this is the circumcenter.
      3. Place the compass at the circumcenter, adjust it so the pencil reaches one of the triangle’s vertices, and draw the circumscribed circle passing through all three vertices.

  4. Label the Key Points

    • Clearly label:
      • The three vertices of the triangle.
      • The perpendicular bisectors of each side.
      • The circumcenter.

  5. Document the Construction

    • Record a video or take a clear picture of your completed construction, making sure to show:
      • The perpendicular bisectors drawn through the sides.
      • The labeled circumcenter.
      • The circumscribed circle passing through all three vertices.

  6. Upload Your Documentation:

    • Upload the video or image of your completed construction to the assignment page. Ensure the file is clear and legible.


Submission Requirements:

  • Video or Image: Ensure your video or image clearly shows the segments, arc marks, circle, and tangents.
  • File Format: Acceptable formats include MP4, MOV, JPG, and PNG.


Grading Criteria:

  • Accuracy: The constructed is a circumscribed circle and goes through all 3 vertices.
  • Clarity: The video or image must clearly show all steps and labels.
  • Markings: Segments, arc marks, perpendicular bisector, circle.
  • Completion: The submission must include all required elements (ray, arc mark, labeled points).


If you have any questions or need further clarification, please contact your instructor. Happy constructing!

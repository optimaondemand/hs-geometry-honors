📋 CONSTRUCTING AN INSCRIBED CIRCLE IN A TRIANGLE

Objective: In this assignment, you will use a provided PDF containing a triangle, print it out, and construct an accurate inscribed circle inside the triangle. You will document your construction process by recording a video or taking a picture, which you will then upload to the assignment page.

Materials Needed:

  • Printed PDF with the provided line segment
  • Compass
  • Straightedge (ruler without measurement markings)
  • Pencil
  • Recording device (camera or smartphone)


Steps to Complete the Assignment:

  1. Print the PDF:

    • Download and print this PDF containing the given triangle.

  2. Prepare Your Tools:

    • Gather your compass, straightedge, and pencil.

  3. Construct the Inscribed Circle

    • Using the construction steps from the lesson, construct the inscribed circle inside the given triangle by following these steps:
      1. Find and construct the angle bisectors of each interior angle of the triangle.
      2. Identify the point where the angle bisectors intersect—this is the incenter.
      3. Draw a perpendicular segment from the incenter to one of the triangle’s sides—this is the radius of the inscribed circle.
      4. Place the compass at the incenter, adjust it to the radius length, and draw the inscribed circle that touches all three sides of the triangle.

  4. Label the Key Points

    • Clearly label:
      • The three vertices of the triangle.
      • The angle bisectors of each interior angle.
      • The incenter.
      • The radius used to draw the inscribed circle.

  5. Document the Construction

    • Record a video or take a clear picture of your completed construction, making sure to show:
      • The angle bisectors drawn through the interior angles.
      • The labeled incenter.
      • The inscribed circle touching all three sides.

  6. Upload Your Documentation:

    • Upload the video or image of your completed construction to the assignment page. Ensure the file is clear and legible.


Submission Requirements:

  • Video or Image: Ensure your video or image clearly shows the segments, arc marks, circle, and tangents.
  • File Format: Acceptable formats include MP4, MOV, JPG, and PNG.


Grading Criteria:

  • Accuracy: The constructed circle is inscribed and touches all 3 sides of the triangle.
  • Clarity: The video or image must clearly show all steps and labels.
  • Markings: Segments, arc marks, angle bisectors, circle.
  • Completion: The submission must include all required elements (ray, arc mark, labeled points).


If you have any questions or need further clarification, please contact your instructor. Happy constructing!
